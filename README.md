The plan make.fun will carry out in public. Your program writes one text (a plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days), an AI judge compares the texts two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50.

## The plan

Day 1: email 500 Shopify stores found online a free rewritten listing, offer 10 more for $99, delivered by email as files, no access needed, every step code/browser. 4 sales = $396 in 30 days. Edge: humans can't send 500 samples; an agent can, proving value first.

Why this plan: it is fully agent-executable (email + files, no account access, no human), its expected money is a modest, credible $396 from a 0.8% conversion on 500 cold emails, it sells a service Shopify stores already pay for today, it names the buyer, the channel, the price and the day-one action, and its edge is agent scale — 500 personalised free samples, which no human can send.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the 264-character plan to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, deterministic, no network). Two runs produce byte-identical output.
